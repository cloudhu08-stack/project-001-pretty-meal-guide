## Facts copied from the official source

- Menu name: TAIAN TABLE MENU, NO.54.
- Menu price: 10 COURSE MENU SELECTION, RMB 2,888 per person.
- The PDF states that the seasonal 10-course degustation menu is fixed and mandatory.
- The black, bold primary dish labels on page 1 form the 10 courses.

- The four add-on dishes and their prices are printed separately on page 2.

## Presentation classifications used in the product

- **Opening / Snacks / Amuse-bouche:** Yangmei Tea, Nicoise Taco, Char Siu Bao, and Caprese. They appear before the first bold primary dish, in that order. The PDF does not name this group.
- **Pre-dessert:** Purple Shiso Sorbet. It appears after the final savory course and immediately before Matcha Gateau. The PDF does not label it “pre-dessert”.
- **Dessert:** Matcha Gateau. It is the final bold course and is followed by small sweet items.
- **Petit Fours:** Bergamot Tart / Peanut Praline, Canele / Coffee Eclair, and Chocolate Box. They appear after Matcha Gateau and are small sweet closing items. The PDF does not label them “petit fours”.

These classifications preserve the published order and make the full meal understandable. They are product-level semantic labels, not quotations of restaurant-defined section names.
